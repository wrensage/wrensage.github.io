A little about my practice

Simple construction.
Thoughtful details.

I work primarily in crochet, creating original amigurumi, accessories, clothing, and textile pieces. My designs are playful and characterful, with an emphasis on approachable construction, considered shaping, and the small details that give a handmade object its personality.

Under the name Wren Sage, I develop original designs and write crochet patterns for other makers. I also run oddly knotted, my handmade shop, where I share finished crochet pieces and a growing collection of unusual little things.

My practice is continually expanding through pattern development, material experiments, and the study of textile techniques. I'm especially interested in the relationship between a design, the materials used to make it, and the person who eventually brings it to life.
