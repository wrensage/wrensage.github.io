Solly the Sun is my first professionally formatted crochet pattern, now available to makers through Ravelry. Solly began as an original amigurumi design, and turning the construction into a pattern has been a new and exciting part of my work.

This release marks a new step in my practice as a crochet designer. I'm looking forward to developing more original patterns and sharing the process along the way.
